# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 1, 2026*

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile and desktop apps published by Luca Soldaini that link to this page (the “Apps”).

## The short version

I do not collect, retain, sell, or share personal information or content from the Apps. The Apps do not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Information collection

The Apps do not send personal information, account information, device identifiers, location data, or content you create to me or to servers I operate. Because I do not receive this information, I do not retain it or use it for any purpose.

Information an App stores remains under your control and is not accessible to me. You can generally remove locally stored information by deleting it in the App or by uninstalling the App.

## Apple analytics

Apple may independently collect information about App Store activity and, when you choose to share it, app usage and diagnostic information. Apple may provide me with aggregated or otherwise non-personally identifying reports through its developer tools. These reports do not allow me to identify you. You can control optional analytics sharing in your device’s privacy settings. Apple handles this information under its [App Analytics & Privacy notice](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-analytics/) and [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children’s privacy

The Apps do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

I may update this policy to reflect changes to the Apps or legal requirements. If an App’s data practices change, I will update this policy or provide an app-specific disclosure before the change takes effect. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date. Changes apply only from the date they are posted.
